Reading: S1 is about 0R before costs except silver 2003-2018 (+0.20R), which fades to zero later; S2 on gold is slightly positive before costs in every period (+0.03 / +0.10 / +0.26R) but costs remove it in development. The coin-flip control is negative even at zero cost: with a limit fill, the conservative fill-hour rule (stop checked, target not) hurts the opposite direction more, because price came from that side within the hour. So 't vs coin flip' for S1 overstates S1; it does not change any verdict, since S1 fails on its own mean R.

## Implementation notes

- A few trades lose far more than 1R (worst gold −107R, silver −386R): weekend or news gaps through stops that were only a few cents wide. Medians are negative too, and S1 is negative after costs in every single year 2004–2026 for both metals.
- S1 decisions where both band edges were touched in the same hour (fill order unknown) are skipped: gold 38, silver 202. The registration did not cover this case.
